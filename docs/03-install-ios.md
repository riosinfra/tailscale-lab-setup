# 03 — Install Tailscale on iOS

## Outcome
Your iPhone is on the tailnet and can reach the droplet and other nodes.

## Prerequisites
- Tailscale account
- iPhone running iOS 15 or later

## Steps

### 1. Install from the App Store
Search Tailscale

### 2. Sign in
Open the app → Get Started → sign in with your Tailscale account.

### 3. Grant VPN permission
iOS will prompt to add a VPN configuration. Tap Allow. 

### 4. Verify
In the Tailscale app tap on the droplet node — it should show Connected.

## Troubleshooting
- **Node not visible:** confirm you signed in with the same Tailscale account on the two seperate devices
